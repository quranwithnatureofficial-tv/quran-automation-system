## Part B: Shorts — Ayat Selection Criteria

### Categories to draw from:
1. **Hope / Comfort / Glad tidings verses** — e.g., verses about Allah's mercy exceeding His wrath, ease after hardship, forgiveness
2. **Prophets' supplications (duas)** — e.g., Prophet Yunus's dua in the whale, Prophet Ibrahim's duas, Prophet Musa's dua for ease
3. **Short narrative/moral verses** that commonly circulate online — e.g., excerpts from the story of the People of the Cave (Ashab al-Kahf), Prophet Yusuf's story, parables
4. **Reflection & Signs of Allah** — verses on creation, nature, sky, earth, rain, mountains, day and night — these pair especially well with the channel's nature-background visual identity

### Verse-combination rule:
Never isolate a verse if doing so changes, weakens, or removes its intended meaning. When necessary, include the surrounding consecutive verses to preserve the complete context — typically this means 2-3 consecutive ayat, but the guiding principle is meaning-preservation, not a fixed count.

### Content rotation (avoid over-weighting emotional content):
Maintain a healthy balance across categories rather than only publishing emotionally intense verses. Suggested rotation across: Hope & Mercy, Dua, Character, Reflection (Signs of Allah), Tawakkul (trust in Allah), Stories, Gratitude.

### Process:
1. Build a master list of candidate ayat/ayat-ranges under the four categories above
2. Tag each with its category, Surah, and ayah range
3. Rotate through this list for daily Shorts, avoiding repeats until the full list has been used once
4. Add new entries over time as the operator identifies more relevant verses

## Part C: Metadata Policy (applies to every video, Short and Long)

Every generated video's database record stores:
- Surah
- Ayah range
- Translation source (and translation revision, per the main architecture doc)
- Theme/category (from Part B's categories, for Shorts)
- Keywords
- Upload date

This supports future search, playlist auto-organization, and analytics without needing to re-derive this information later.
